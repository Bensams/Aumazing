Gemini prompt — for a proper rest pose
Her current artwork works as-is, but it's an open-mouth smile with arms slightly out, which makes an odd neutral rest that every other action inherits. Worth generating a dedicated one. Attach Lexianne_chibi.png:


Using the attached chibi character as the exact reference, produce a single
clean full-body REST pose of the same girl.

IDENTITY: keep her exactly as drawn — same face, same large brown eyes, same
long dark wavy hair, same white puff-sleeve dress, same thin gold necklace,
same white strappy sandals, same flat 2D cartoon anime chibi style with thick
clean outlines and soft cel shading.

POSE: standing straight and centered, facing the viewer, both arms relaxed
down at her sides, hands empty and relaxed, feet flat on the ground, calm
friendly CLOSED-MOUTH smile, eyes open looking at the viewer. A neutral
resting pose she could return to after any gesture.

FRAMING: full body from the top of her hair to the soles of her shoes, all
inside the frame with clear empty margin above her head and below her feet.
Do not crop any part of her. Centered horizontally. Portrait 3:4.

BACKGROUND: plain solid pure white, completely empty. No shadow under her, no
floor, no gradient, no props, no text, no logos or brand marks, no watermark,
no extra characters.
Why each constraint matters, from what actually bit us:

Closed mouth, arms down — the rest pose is the first frame of all nine clips. Whatever she's doing here, she does in every sheet.
Pure white, no shadow — background removal floods inward from the frame border. A drop shadow survives it and shows as grey mush at her feet. Her white dress is safe; it's enclosed by dark outlines so it never touches the border.
Margin on all sides — this was the bug that cost ~1,800 credits. Tight framing made seedance zoom in and crop the feet off 13 sheets.
